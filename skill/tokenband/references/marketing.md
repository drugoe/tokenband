# Advertising and marketing

Record the business question, audience, channel/account, period/time zone,
campaign scope, KPI definitions and permitted actions. Use folder-owned data and
rules; do not borrow another account's restrictions, working data or authority.
Apply text and graphics rules to creative materials and engineering rules to
code, databases, infrastructure and access.

Common roles, supported efforts, consent and review counts come only from [routing](routing.md). Use [communication](communication.md) for bounded source/evidence packets; apply subject checks to their own parts. Keep the folder's business authority and narrower action permissions.

Validate source/export time, complete periods, conversion delay, filters,
denominators, attribution and mapping between platforms and analytics. For each
material number supply the comparison base and reproducible calculation.
Distinguish observations, explanatory hypotheses and recommendations. A single
before/after change does not establish causality; check plausible alternatives.
Verify current platform rules and applicable legal requirements when relevant.

Recommendations specify target objects, mechanism, success metric, observation
period, assumptions, expected spend, risks and rollback. Budgets and business
goals require accepted human decisions; estimates remain estimates. Independent
QA recalculates key numbers and checks evidence, logic, limitations, claims and
experiment design. Critical financial/access/irreversible changes use the shared
critical review gate, not a model-specific exception.

State each proposed action's execution duration as well as its observation period; include stop/rollback conditions and the decision point. Assess aggregate financial/access/reversibility risk across the whole recommendation: splitting actions or preparing exact values cannot reduce its gate. Preserve estimates, uncertainty and causal limits in compact reports.

Before changing active campaigns, bids, budgets, ads or keywords, prepare exact
IDs, old/new values, scope, verification and rollback. Execute when the action
is already authorized; otherwise ask about the concrete prepared action. An
analysis request alone does not authorize campaign changes or publication.
Preserve narrower local consent requirements. Save results and verify actual
settings after an authorized change.

Map claims/requirements to actual source snapshots, calculations, final assets or verified settings with verified/failed/unknown outcomes. Use [lifecycle](lifecycle.md#engineering-and-acceptance) for substantive finding dispositions, focused rechecks and intake under the common independent gate. Analysis completion grants neither acceptance nor action authority.
