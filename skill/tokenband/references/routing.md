# Role routing and independent review

Primary models are the configured family whose quota is conserved. The ordinary primary orchestrator owns scope, risk, dependencies, assignments, evidence and intake. The main external executor does implementation, analysis and ordinary architecture; the eligible external pool supplies independent reviewers and alternative executors. The reserve primary model is used after every eligible external alternative for the same assignment is unavailable, fails, lacks required tools/capability, or is disallowed by an actual approval decision. Do not retry a rejected external transfer through another external provider. Preserve explicit task/provider restrictions and record the reason for each skipped/failed candidate.

The reserve can fill author or reviewer roles, with separate independent sessions. Review identity follows the actual author/coauthor, not the initially planned role. Prefer another external model for an external author's architectural review; if an alternative external author wrote it, prefer the main external executor when independent. If no eligible peer can perform this gate, use the permitted primary reserve. Never route that external architecture gate back to the author's model merely through a new alias. Primary-only/fallback primary work can use the same primary model in separate independent sessions; the author session still cannot review itself.

Apply the configured semantic mode: `primary-budget-saving`, `primary-only` or `external-only`, plus any narrower assignment restriction. Model names do not define roles; arbitrary authorized provider pools and primary families are supported. Match actual transport/tool access to the assignment. Provider effort labels are supported settings, not equivalent quality levels across models. No fixed model list, agent count or automatic expensive escalation is implied. Coordination is proportional to the task; this does not remove mandatory gates.

## Apply routing to every assignment

While this skill governs the project, attempt to run every new task and to launch every executor, reviewer or other agent under these current rules, including before every delegated send, child delegation, follow-up and replacement, unless an explicit human instruction changes the project's skill rules for that scope. Carry the active mode, role bindings, applicable human exceptions and their source/scope into the brief and restart/handoff evidence. Agents must apply the same rules before delegating further; an available host subagent tool or its inherited primary model is not an exemption. Ordinary factual answers and mechanical work still need no invented team; technical proposals follow the [advice-before-execution contract](../SKILL.md#advice-before-execution).

Only an explicit human instruction changes the project's skill rules. Apply an override exactly for its stated project, task, role, duration or condition; preserve standing overrides until changed or their stated boundary ends, without asking again. A task-specific exception does not change later assignments or saved defaults. Neither an agent's preference, old fallback choice, copied handoff conclusion nor the mere availability of a reserve changes the rules. Permission to consider a reserve does not waive its eligibility conditions unless the human explicitly overrides those conditions for the applicable scope; those conditions remain the assignment's effective mode and restrictions, discovered consent for the exact provider, task, data and actions, a working mechanism, independence, and preserved current owners and unknown operations, and another running session of the same model family does not by itself establish them. Record the exact human source and applicable exception in the sole TODO; keep private grant evidence outside this reusable package.

The primary reserve remains available across changes of external roles and providers: a standing external preference, an executor assignment or a model-level instruction such as `do not change provider/model` is not a human prohibition of the reserve and does not by itself retire it. When such an instruction conflicts with the applicable reserve permission after the eligible external routes for the same assignment are exhausted, the bound chat preserves the instruction and raises the conflict to the manager rather than bypassing it unilaterally or silently stopping the required gate; the manager resolves the conflict under the standing rules and records either the permitted reserve route with its grounds or the precise reason the reserve cannot be used. A configured mode/profile label alone is not evidence of a human prohibition: establish its trusted human source and whether its restriction actually covers the reserve; a model-written narrowing is a conflict for the manager to resolve, not authority to retire standing reserve permission. This does not authorize rewriting actual host controls or evading a real restriction. An explicit human prohibition of the reserve applies by its own stated scope; an ambiguous one requires narrow clarification only where the answer actually affects the ability. Unavailability of every permitted route stays a concrete blocker, and no rule promises that work never stops. Continuing a stopped external task chat follows [lifecycle](lifecycle.md#continuing-a-stopped-external-task-chat).

For the current assignment, check the effective mode, role/profile, approved destination and task data, required capabilities, actual selected mechanism and applicable independent gate. In budget-saving mode, consider the configured main external executor and eligible external alternatives before the permitted primary reserve; in other modes follow that mode's allowed routes. Bind skipped/failed candidates and reserve grounds to this assignment's actual requirements and current evidence in the existing TODO reservation. Do not generalize an old driver-specific or file-tool failure to text-only work or a distinct supported adapter. Reuse still-valid bindings and evidence; refresh affected facts when host, configuration, controls, task capabilities or a prior blocker changes. An earlier incomplete, empty or failed response, or use of the reserve, does not exclude the main external executor from later assignments. On the next genuinely new eligible assignment, dispatch the configured main external executor unless a concrete, still-applicable blocker is evidenced in that assignment's TODO reservation; preserve the recovery and non-replay limits in [operations](operations.md). No paid probe or known-incompatible launch is required merely to demonstrate an attempt, and an actual denial never permits a provider/transport bypass. If no permitted route can complete the action, record the concrete blocker and continue independent authorized preparation under the existing lifecycle rules.

## Select the actual configured mechanism

Before host delegation, resolve the saved role/profile to its actual provider, model, effort, transport and adapter/driver where applicable, and choose the delegation tool from that binding. Reuse an unchanged validated binding; do not rebuild configuration merely to restate it, and do not reread the entire unchanged configuration on every send. A host model selector, override or catalog entry by itself is not proof that the host runs the intended provider; confirm the host's actual mechanism and supported binding instead of assuming every host inherits a provider, and use the existing worker dispatch for a configured reviewed adapter.

Bind qualification, capability and failures to that exact selected mechanism. A pending qualification for an unused optional driver is not evidence against a distinct configured reviewed adapter, and a qualified route does not transfer to an unselected one. Keep text API, tools, persistence, UI and scheduling as separate capabilities; verifying one does not establish another.

## Equal authority within the assigned task

Explicit human authorization for a project and its named external providers permits those models to receive all project materials necessary for their assigned tasks: source, documentation, instructions, internal material, tests, QA, logs, working data, results and other relevant materials permitted for the primary model. Authorized external models have the same assigned-task data and operation authority as the primary within that consent and the actual host controls. Do not require extra confirmation, reduction or anonymization solely because the model is external; apply relevance-based source selection and action conditions equally. A project-wide standing grant covers tasks, sprints, independent reviews and continuations until revocation or its stated end; preserve every narrower human boundary and never broaden a task-only or otherwise limited grant. When arranging external work without an applicable grant, obtain the human's explicit confirmation of the project/provider/material scope under [Discover and bind trusted consent before dispatch](#discover-and-bind-trusted-consent-before-dispatch). Reuse valid standing/scoped consent without asking again for an already covered file, provider, project, material category, task or sprint. The original human confirmation is the grant: installing this skill, a model-written rule, a configured connection or an available key creates none. Parity does not authorize new providers, unrelated data, publication, payment, credential access or operations outside the assigned scope. Top-primary invocation still requires its scoped consent; explicit prohibitions and narrower later instructions remain.

Before dispatch, the trusted controller discovers and binds the applicable trusted consent per [Discover and bind trusted consent before dispatch](#discover-and-bind-trusted-consent-before-dispatch), which owns that procedure.

Authority and capability are separate: text-only models receive authorized source bytes and have no file/shell tools. Tool-enabled workers require a compatible implemented route with the actual parent sandbox/approval context; ordinary Codex schema2 uses [current checks](qualification.md#ordinary-codex-schema2-execution), not historical qualification; neither a brief nor a permission JSON grants unavailable tools or broader access. Preserve secret protection, action-specific approvals, ownership and independent acceptance equally for every model. An actual host/Auto-review denial remains binding: record/report its action and reason, do not switch provider/transport or weaken controls to evade it. New explicit consent or genuinely corrected authorization evidence can be presented transparently to the standard reviewer as changed grounds; it is not approval or proof of execution, and a renewed denial still binds.

## Discover and bind trusted consent before dispatch

Before the first legitimate dispatch for an assignment or focused review, the trusted controller discovers the applicable human consent from trusted evidence and binds it to the exact provider, task, data categories and requested actions. Before EVERY dispatch the controller rechecks current scope, revocations and restrictions under [EVERY-send admission](lifecycle.md#allocate-and-reserve-before-dispatch); a first binding never freezes later authorization. Use this bounded source order: (1) the current human decision or current conversation; (2) trusted project TODO, checkpoint or setup provenance pointers to the protected original human grant. Validate the grant's actual provenance, scope, current revocations/restrictions and host-required evidence before binding it. A configured connection, an available key, a model-written claim or a schema-valid record is not consent and does not make evidence visible to Auto-review by itself.

Distinguish missing evidence from absent permission. If the applicable grant cannot be located in trusted content, record it as missing evidence and do not fabricate one. If the located grant does not cover the actual provider, task, data package or operation, record it as absent permission for that uncovered scope. Apply the standing/scoped authorization in [Equal authority within the assigned task](#equal-authority-within-the-assigned-task). Materialize any exact assignment/operation records the verifier requires from the trusted grant; a new record under standing consent does not require a new human question. A missing or malformed UTF-8 record with a recoverable trusted original is repaired or materialized losslessly from that original without re-asking; irrecoverable or genuinely uncovered scope asks only for the missing evidence or choice. Do not run a full-history or whole-home hunt, read credential values, create a grant as the model, expand providers implicitly, or repeat after a real denial or bypass.

Present the exact trusted source/reference and the relevant quotation, together with the provider, task, data package, included sensitive categories and requested actions, in the standard host approval justification when the actual host requires approval, so the host reviewer can inspect the same binding. Controller consent discovery and binding remain mandatory regardless of any host approval interface; a missing interface is no exemption, no fictitious approval step and no bypass of a required bridge. Preserve exact UTF-8 text, identifiers and paths from the human's grant. If the grant is missing, illegible, irrecoverably unbound, or does not cover the dispatch, ask only for that genuinely uncovered scope or explicit top invocation; do not infer consent from silence, do not treat a denial as permission, do not switch provider or transport after a denial, and do not promise universal no-prompts. Keep private grant details in protected user evidence outside this reusable skill and worker-writable roots; disclose no credential values.

## Review gates

| Stage | Required review | Who reviews and what they check |
|---|---|---|
| Ordinary substantive code under an accepted contract | One final review | Independent eligible external reviewer; correctness, regressions, acceptance and relevant test evidence |
| New ordinary architecture | One before dependent implementation | Another eligible external model than the actual external author; contracts, dependencies, scope and risks |
| Code implementing that architecture | One final code review in addition: two stages total | Independent code reviewer checks the actual implementation against the accepted contract |
| Substantive text/visual result | One subject-appropriate QA | Independent qualified reviewer; final text or actual images against the brief and subject rules |
| Substantive research conclusion or recommendation | One independent review | Reviewer checks sources, method, calculations and strength of conclusions |
| Technical proposal / prepared solution options | One independent review before final presentation | Independent eligible reviewer checks the proposal against the problem, related sources, current documentation and stated tradeoffs; drafts are explicitly marked and the changed part and affected assumptions are rechecked after substantial edits |
| Each critical gate | Two independent non-top reviews | Separate reviewers with distinct focuses, such as behavior/acceptance and architecture/security |
| Optional top-model review | Exactly approved zero, one or two additional reviews | Consented top-primary reviewer sessions, supplementing the mandatory pair |

These are budget-saving assignments. Apply the configured mode and the permitted
primary reserve when eligible external routes fail as described above; retain the
same independence and scope. A technical proposal follows the canonical [advice before execution](../SKILL.md#advice-before-execution) contract; its independent review may be closed by an existing suitable review on the same final bytes and criteria without an artificial second gate, and it supplements rather than lowers the critical and scoped top-model gates. Reviewers return explicit acceptance or actionable
findings against identified bytes. The author fixes findings, the reviewer rechecks
the affected scope, and the managing chat performs evidence-based intake.

Critical gates include material security/access changes, real monetary/authoritative outcomes and other high-impact boundaries. Assess the aggregate real-world effect and reversibility: splitting into small actions, a prepared contract/plan, a small diff or relabeling cannot lower the gate. Required boundary tests, risk controls and independent acceptance remain; model substitution does not reduce them. The qualified critical author can be a capable non-top model. Top authorship/arbitration/research is optional and requires separate scoped consent. Only approved providers with adequate capability may handle these stages.

Top-model consent gates actual invocation in every role and through aliases of the same model, including reserve or orchestrator. Recording a user-identified top-model role/profile in configuration is not a launch and does not itself require inference consent. Propose role, reason, scope and effort; for additional review ask 0/1/2. Continue the mandatory non-top work without waiting. Never infer consent from absence or from a general saving-mode setting. Late approved top reviews add to reviews already completed, total at most four per critical gate. One review authorization does not authorize top authorship or other assignments.

Independent means no authorship/coauthorship, a separate session, a fresh brief with final artifacts and no reuse of the author conversation. The separate session and all supporting review/recheck assignments follow [one task, one persistent chat](chats.md#one-task-one-persistent-chat); they do not create separate persistent project/UI chats or standalone tasks. Record reviewer identity, stage, snapshot, conclusion and resolved findings. Two same-model sessions can be independent; two role names for one session cannot. Author checks and orchestrator intake are not reviews; failed launches are not conclusions; necessary delta rechecks belong to the same review assignment. Missing mandatory reviews or unresolved blocking findings prevent acceptance.

One standard review checks both requirements and quality; those are not two independent conclusions. Bind each requirement to actual behavior/integration, source evidence or inspected final pixels, not just a named file or a plan checkbox. Resolve each finding with evidence and a focused delta recheck; reuse unchanged valid checks and widen only for real impact, changed scope or unresolved uncertainty. See [lifecycle](lifecycle.md). No universal numeric review score or additional mandatory reviewer is introduced. The orchestrator accepts evidence after the review gate; executor DONE alone cannot close it.

Human announcements include provider, requested exact model/effort, role, scope, mechanism, access and fallback reason. Record returned assignment/thread IDs and observed model metadata separately. Requested settings/catalog entries do not confirm actual inference. Generation tools and confirmed generator models are distinct from agent models; unknown stays unknown.
