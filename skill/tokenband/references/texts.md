# Texts and documents

For dedicated writing or substantive editing, record purpose, audience, channel,
requested language, sources of truth, accepted claims and acceptance criteria.
Ordinary factual chat replies and mechanical typo fixes need no separate production
package or reviewer. Technical proposals in chat still follow [advice before execution](../SKILL.md#advice-before-execution). Follow the relevant installed document skill when producing
a formatted artifact; inspect the exported result when layout matters.

Use [communication](communication.md) for concise briefs/results and selective source packets. Common role routing, effort support, consent and review counts come only from [routing](routing.md); this subject adds content checks. Keep applicable local audience and format constraints.

Distinguish deterministic replacement/export, bounded editorial judgment and new meaning/facts/claims/concepts when selecting configured effort and capability. Preserve local minimum efforts and exact task/work-type identifiers. For mixed material, split coherent scopes or use a route qualified for the most demanding part; never split to lower aggregate risk. Effort labels are not cross-model quality equivalences.

Separate source facts, exact quotations, interpretations and proposals. Preserve
meaning, numbers, constraints and requested terminology. Verify material factual
claims against applicable sources; check current primary sources for rules,
specifications or other changeable facts. Authorization to publish a claim is
not proof that the claim is true. Do not invent missing product facts, citations
or legal guarantees. Mark unresolved claims and drafts explicitly.

Preserve negatives, exceptions, units, identifiers, exact quotations and material uncertainty when shortening. Associate each material claim or required section with its source and final artifact/location. Include inspected source versions, actual authors, requested versus observed execution identity, technical/content checks and limitations in the compact result; do not infer truth from approval to use a claim.

Subject QA checks final factual support, meaning, completeness, audience,
language, consistency and conformity to the brief under [routing's independent
gate](routing.md). Use [lifecycle](lifecycle.md#engineering-and-acceptance) for
identified final inputs, finding dispositions and focused rechecks. Layout QA
inspects the actual final export, not just source/exporter success. Draft
acceptance may precede QA only when clearly marked draft and unpublished.
Writing/acceptance grants no sending or publication authority.
